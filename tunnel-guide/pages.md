title: Making a page someone will read
refs: pages/kit-blocks, pages/charts-and-numbers
---
Pick the shape from what the reader will do with the page, not from its topic:
- notes: capture many small things and find the important ones again.
- brief: get the answer and make a decision.
- timeline: see what happens when, and what is stuck.
- board: see every option at once before choosing.
- numbers: understand what the money or metrics say.
- story: care about one idea and remember it.
- rendition: read a fuller account, chapter by chapter.

Then:
1. templates find with your intent gives the best three; templates get gives one template's skeleton, slots and finish line.
2. Fill the slots with the reader's own material and keep the structure. Seed it as HTML with template set to its id.
3. Run templates check on the uri before you call it done.

The page rules, one line each:
- Every heading is a sentence that states the finding.
- The answer comes first, then three reasons, then the detail.
- A Setup list defines every actor, thing and number before it is used.
- One accent colour marks the one thing that carries the message.
- Every figure has a unit, a source line and a takeaway.
- Mark estimates and illustrative numbers next to the number.
- Short sentences, active voice, no emoji.
- It reads at 400px wide and in dark mode.

Keep the two kit tags the template links, and add no other stylesheet, script or font; the check refuses anything external. The blocks the kit draws are in pages/kit-blocks; type, colour and chart rules are in pages/charts-and-numbers.
